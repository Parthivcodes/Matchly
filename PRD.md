## Executive Summary & Clarifying Decisions

Matchly is an automated, cloud-native **3-Way Invoice Matching SaaS** engineered for physical goods businesses (distributors, manufacturers, omnichannel retailers, healthcare facilities) processing 200 to 10,000 invoices per month. 

Current AP solutions force mid-sized companies to choose between error-prone manual spreadsheet checks or multi-million-dollar, year-long enterprise ERP deployments (SAP VIM, Coupa). Matchly delivers **deterministic line-level 3-way matching** (Vendor Invoice ↔ Purchase Order ↔ Goods Receipt Note) within 30 minutes of onboarding at accessible SMB price points ($49–$499/month).

### Baseline Architectural Assumptions
1. **Tax Model:** Flexible multi-tax engine supporting Sales Tax, VAT, and GST (CGST/SGST/IGST with HSN codes). Tax mismatches are isolated from base price mismatches.
2. **AI Extraction Strategy:** Direct multimodal LLM extraction (Structured Outputs with JSON Schema + bounding boxes) with deterministic post-validation fallback.
3. **GRN Capture:** Dual mode — batch CSV import from legacy WMS/ERP OR a lightweight, 2-click digital receiving screen for warehouse staff.
4. **Tolerance Hierarchy:** Hierarchical rule engine: `Vendor Rule` > `Category Rule` > `Tenant Global Default`. Enforces both Unit Price Variance and Quantity Variance.
5. **Currency Policy:** MVP enforces matching within identical currencies (`Invoice.currency == PO.currency`). Multi-currency conversion via FX rate tables deferred to v1.1.

## 1. Problem Statement & Target Customer Pain Points

### 1.1 The Industry Problem
Organizations that procure physical inventory receive three distinct commercial records for every transaction:
1. **The Purchase Order (PO):** The commercial agreement generated by procurement specifying items, quantities, agreed unit pricing, and payment terms.
2. **The Goods Receipt Note (GRN / Receiving Slip):** Warehouse evidence recording physical counts delivered, damaged goods, or partial shipments.
3. **The Vendor Invoice:** The bill requesting financial settlement.

Today, 82% of mid-market businesses execute this reconciliation manually across paper receipts, PDF email attachments, and spreadsheet formulas.

### 1.2 Quantitative Impact of Manual AP in SMBs
* **Direct Financial Leakage:** Erroneous, duplicate, or unverified disbursements consume **0.5% to 2.1% of annual AP spend**. For a $10M revenue distributor, this represents $35,000 to $150,000 in unrecoverable annual cash drain.
* **Overbilling & Price Creep:** Vendors invoice prices 2–7% higher than negotiated PO contracts, betting that volume blinds manual invoice checkers.
* **Phantom Shipments & Short-Delivery Billing:** Suppliers bill for 100% of the PO while delivering only 80%, leaving the buyer paying for undelivered stock.
* **Duplicate Invoice Payments:** Identical invoices resubmitted with minor variations (e.g., `INV-1092` vs `INV1092`, or altered dates) pass through manual ledger review.
* **Operational Sluggishness:** AP clerks spend 6 to 12 minutes per invoice manually cross-referencing lines, chasing warehouse managers on WhatsApp/Slack, and re-keying data into accounting software.

### 1.3 Target Customers & Vertical Pain Points
| Vertical | Order Profile | Key Operational Vulnerability |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Wholesale Distributors** | High-velocity, hundreds of SKUs per PO, frequent backorders. | Partial shipments split over 3–5 deliveries; vendors invoice in full before remaining stock arrives. |
| **Light Manufacturers** | Raw materials, fluctuating steel/chemical prices, weight discrepancies. | Unit of Measure (UoM) discrepancies and surcharges (freight, packaging) hidden in line items. |
| **Omnichannel Retail** | High volume, cross-dock deliveries, rapid vendor turnover. | Duplicate invoices submitted across multiple store locations or billing channels. |
| **Hospitals & Clinics** | Consumables, surgical supplies, pharmaceutical lots. | Strict compliance needs; paying for unverified batches creates cash drain and regulatory risks. |

## 5. Functional Requirements (User Stories & Acceptance Criteria)

### FR1: Multi-Tenant Authentication & Organization Structure
* **US1.1:** *As an Administrator, I can register a new organization, define our base currency, and invite team members by email with designated roles.*
  * **Given** an unauthenticated user on the signup page,
  * **When** they submit organization name, admin email, password, and base currency,
  * **Then** create the tenant record, hash the password using Argon2id/bcrypt, create the admin membership, and log an audit event.
  * **Acceptance:**
    * Email invitations contain cryptographically secure tokens expiring in 7 days.
    * Every SQL query in the application must enforce `WHERE tenant_id = :current_tenant_id` (via database-level row filtering or session context).

### FR2: Master Data Management (Vendors, POs, GRNs)
* **US2.1:** *As an AP Clerk or Admin, I can upload CSV files for Vendors, Purchase Orders, and Goods Receipts with instant column mapping and validation.*
  * **Validation Rules:**
    * `PO Number` must be unique per vendor within the tenant.
    * PO Lines must contain `item_code`, `description`, `ordered_qty` ($> 0$), `unit_price` ($\ge 0$), and `tax_rate` ($\ge 0$).
    * GRN must reference an existing `po_number` and `po_line_number` or `item_code`.
    * Import is idempotent: re-uploading an existing PO or GRN file updates records without creating duplicates.
* **US2.2:** *As a Warehouse Worker, I can open any open PO on a tablet or desktop and record physical receipts with received quantities, receiving date, and damage notes.*
  * **Acceptance:** Creating a GRN recalculates `cumulative_received_qty` on the PO line in real-time.

### FR3: Invoice Ingestion & Document Pre-processing
* **US3.1:** *As an AP Clerk, I can drag and drop up to 50 PDF, PNG, or JPG invoice files simultaneously.*
  * **File Constraints:** Maximum file size 15 MB per document; rejects password-protected PDFs; rejects corrupted binaries with immediate user notification.
  * **Storage:** Files are encrypted with AES-256 and stored in an S3-compatible private bucket. Access is strictly through short-lived presigned URLs (15-minute expiration).

### FR4: AI Extraction, Confidence Scoring & Verification UI
* **US4.1:** *As an AP Clerk, I see the original document in a split-screen viewer with all extracted fields displayed on an interactive editing form.*
  * **Extracted Schema:**
    1. Header: Vendor Name, Vendor Tax ID/GSTIN, Invoice Number, Invoice Date, Due Date, Currency, PO Reference Number.
    2. Financials: Subtotal, Tax Total, Discount Total, Grand Total.
    3. Line Items: Line Number, Item/SKU Code, Description, Quantity, Unit of Measure, Unit Price, Tax Rate, Line Total.
  * **Confidence Scoring:** Every extracted field returns a confidence float $\in [0.00, 1.00]$.
    * Fields with confidence $< 0.85$ are highlighted in amber.
    * Missing required fields (e.g., Invoice Number, Total) are highlighted in red.
  * **Arithmetic Cross-Check:**
    $$\sum (\text{Line Total}) \stackrel{?}{=} \text{Subtotal}$$
    $$\text{Subtotal} + \text{Tax} - \text{Discount} \stackrel{?}{=} \text{Grand Total}$$
    If arithmetic variance $> 0.05$ (allowing for standard rounding), display an inline arithmetic warning banner.

### FR5: 3-Way Line-Level Matching Engine
* **US5.1:** *As the System, when an invoice is confirmed, I execute deterministic 3-way matching across PO, GRNs, and Invoice lines.*
  * **Algorithm Logic (Detailed in Section 8):**
    1. Resolve PO by exact or normalized PO reference.
    2. Aggregate all GRNs registered against that PO.
    3. Aggregate all previously approved/paid invoice lines for that PO.
    4. Compute available billable quantity:
       $$\text{Billable Quantity} = \sum \text{GRN Quantity} - \sum \text{Prior Invoiced Quantity}$$
    5. Evaluate Unit Price Variance against PO Unit Price.
    6. Evaluate Quantity Variance against Billable Quantity.
    7. Compare variances against active tolerance limits.
  * **Outcome Classifications:**
    * `MATCHED_EXACT`: 100% price and quantity agreement on all lines.
    * `MATCHED_TOLERANCE`: All lines within active percentage/absolute tolerances.
    * `EXCEPTION`: One or more lines violate tolerances or validation rules.

### FR6: Duplicate & Fraud Detection
* **US6.1:** *As a Finance Controller, I want the system to intercept duplicate invoices before any matching occurs.*
  * **Level 1 (Exact Collision):** `tenant_id` + `vendor_id` + `normalized(invoice_number)`.
    * *Action:* Instant hard rejection with reason code `DUPLICATE_EXACT`.
  * **Level 2 (Fuzzy Collision):** Same vendor AND exact Grand Total AND invoice date within $\pm 45$ days, OR invoice numbers matching with Levenshtein distance $\le 1$ (e.g. `INV-901` vs `INV901`).
    * *Action:* Route to queue with reason code `DUPLICATE_SUSPECTED` and display side-by-side comparison of prior invoice.

### FR7: Exception Management & Resolution Workflows
* **US7.1:** *As an AP Reviewer, I can inspect flagged exceptions, review line-by-line diffs, and resolve them.*
  * **Available Resolution Actions:**
    1. **Approve / Override:** Authorized user approves with a mandatory, auditable textual reason code (e.g., *"Price increase pre-approved via email with Marcus"*).
    2. **Reject Invoice:** Marks invoice as rejected, generates a standard vendor rejection notice detailing line discrepancies.
    3. **Request Credit Note:** Marks as pending credit note; links credit note when received to balance the overage.
    4. **Re-Run Match:** Triggered after warehouse staff submits an updated GRN or procurement issues a revised PO.

### FR8: Amount-Threshold Approvals
* **US8.1:** *As an Admin, I can configure approval routing rules based on gross invoice value.*
  * **Rule Tiers (Default Config):**
    * Tier 1: $\le \$2,500$ and `MATCHED_EXACT` $\rightarrow$ Auto-Approved for export.
    * Tier 2: $\$2,500.01 - \$10,000$ $\rightarrow$ Requires AP Clerk or Procurement Sign-off.
    * Tier 3: $> \$10,000$ or any `MATCHED_TOLERANCE` / Overridden Exception $\rightarrow$ Requires Finance Controller approval.

### FR9: Dashboards & Financial Analytics
* **US9.1:** *As a Controller or CFO, I have real-time visibility into operational AP metrics and cash leakage prevention.*
  * **Widgets:**
    1. **Match Rate Funnel:** Auto-matched vs Toleranced vs Exceptions.
    2. **Exception Breakdown Bar Chart:** Grouped by reason codes.
    3. **Cash Protected KPI Card:** Total dollar variance blocked from overbilling and duplicate submissions.
    4. **AP Aging Tracker:** Invoices awaiting review ($< 3$ days, $3-7$ days, $> 7$ days).

### FR10: Immutable Audit Logging
* **US10.1:** *As an Auditor, I can review an unalterable log of every state change, document upload, OCR edit, matching execution, and user override.*
  * Every audit entry records: `id`, `tenant_id`, `user_id`, `action`, `entity_type`, `entity_id`, `before_state (JSONB)`, `after_state (JSONB)`, `ip_address`, `user_agent`, and `timestamp_utc`.

## 6. Comprehensive Tolerance & Exception Reason Architecture

### 6.1 Tolerance Hierarchy & Evaluation Logic
Matchly implements a 3-tier cascade for tolerance evaluation:
1. **Vendor-Specific Rule:** (e.g., Vendor "Global Steel" has 0% price tolerance, 5% weight quantity tolerance).
2. **Category-Specific Rule:** (e.g., Category "Bulk Fasteners" has 2% price, 3% quantity tolerance).
3. **Tenant Global Default:** (e.g., 1.5% price tolerance, 0% quantity tolerance).

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────┐
│       Tolerance Parameters           │
├──────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. Price Tolerance Percentage (%)    │
│ 2. Price Tolerance Absolute ($)      │
│ 3. Quantity Tolerance Percentage (%) │
│ 4. Quantity Tolerance Absolute (Qty) │
│ 5. Total Variance Threshold ($)      │
└──────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Evaluation Formula:**  
For a given invoice line with quantity $Q_{inv}$, unit price $P_{inv}$, PO unit price $P_{po}$, and billable quantity $Q_{billable}$:

$$\Delta P = P_{inv} - P_{po}$$
$$\Delta Q = Q_{inv} - Q_{billable}$$

* If $\Delta P \le 0$: Favorable price variance $\rightarrow$ **PASS (Informational Log)**.
* If $\Delta P > 0$: Unfavorable price variance $\rightarrow$
  $$\text{Price Passed} \iff \left(\Delta P \le P_{po} \times \frac{\text{Tol}_{price\%}}{100}\right) \mathbf{\lor} \left(\Delta P \le \text{Tol}_{price\_abs}\right)$$
  *(Mode configured per tenant: Stricter [AND] vs Permissive [OR]; default is Permissive).*
* If $\Delta Q \le 0$: Line within received quantity $\rightarrow$ **PASS**.
* If $\Delta Q > 0$: Overbilled quantity $\rightarrow$
  $$\text{Qty Passed} \iff \left(\Delta Q \le Q_{billable} \times \frac{\text{Tol}_{qty\%}}{100}\right) \mathbf{\lor} \left(\Delta Q \le \text{Tol}_{qty\_abs}\right)$$

### 6.2 Standardized Exception Reason Codes
| Exception Code | Severity | Trigger Condition | Recommended Corrective Action |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| `PO_NOT_FOUND` | High | No matching PO found from invoice reference or vendor metadata. | AP Clerk manually links PO or requests PO number from vendor. |
| `PO_CLOSED` | Medium | Invoiced PO is already marked fully invoiced or cancelled. | Procurement reviews if PO should be re-opened. |
| `ITEM_NOT_ON_PO` | High | Extracted line SKU or description has no match on the PO. | Reject line or obtain procurement change order. |
| `GRN_MISSING` | Critical | PO exists, but zero GRNs have been recorded for the line. | Contact warehouse to verify if shipment has arrived. |
| `QTY_BILLED_EXCEEDS_RECEIVED` | Critical | Invoiced quantity exceeds cumulative billable GRN quantity. | Short payment: pay only received qty or await pending shipment. |
| `QTY_BILLED_EXCEEDS_ORDERED` | High | Cumulative invoiced quantity exceeds total ordered on PO. | Procurement authorization needed for over-delivery. |
| `PRICE_HIGHER` | High | Invoice unit price exceeds PO price beyond allowed tolerance. | Request corrected invoice or vendor credit note. |
| `TOTAL_MISMATCH` | Medium | Sum of line items does not equal invoice header Grand Total. | Clerk corrects line extraction or freight/surcharge line. |
| `TAX_MISMATCH` | Medium | Calculated tax rate/amount diverges from PO tax configuration. | Finance Controller reviews tax jurisdiction applicability. |
| `CURRENCY_MISMATCH` | High | Invoice currency does not equal PO currency. | Hold for currency conversion verification. |
| `DUPLICATE_EXACT` | Critical | Exact match on Vendor + Invoice Number in tenant records. | Hard block. Reject immediately. |
| `DUPLICATE_SUSPECTED` | High | Same vendor, same amount, dates within 45 days. | Review prior invoice side-by-side to verify separate event. |
| `LOW_EXTRACTION_CONFIDENCE` | Low | AI confidence score $< 0.85$ on one or more mandatory fields. | Clerk verifies highlighted fields prior to match execution. |

## 7. Non-Functional Requirements (NFRs)

### 7.1 Security & Multi-Tenancy Architecture
* **Strict Logical Separation:** Multi-tenancy enforced at every database interaction. In PostgreSQL, all domain tables include `tenant_id UUID NOT NULL REFERENCES tenants(id)`.
* **Row-Level Security (RLS):** PostgreSQL RLS policies applied with session-variable scoping (`SET LOCAL app.current_tenant_id = '...'`). No query can cross-pollinate customer data.
* **Encryption Standards:**
  * In Transit: TLS 1.3 enforced on all public and internal service traffic.
  * At Rest: AES-256 GCM encryption on database block volumes and object storage buckets.
* **Document Security:** S3 buckets maintain private access policies. Document preview images and PDFs are streamed exclusively through time-limited presigned URLs (TTL $\le 900$ seconds).
* **Vulnerability Defenses:** Built-in protection against OWASP Top 10: parameterized queries (zero SQL injection), strict Content Security Policy (CSP), CSRF protection, and rate limiting (Redis token bucket: 100 req/min for web, 20 req/min for auth endpoints).

### 7.2 Data Privacy & LLM Governance
* **Zero Model Training Retention:** Matchly uses enterprise LLM APIs (Google Cloud Gemini / Anthropic / OpenAI) governed by **Zero Data Retention (ZDR)** agreements. Customer invoice documents and data are **never** utilized to train public or private foundational models.
* **Compliance Alignment:** Engineered to satisfy SOC 2 Type II controls, India Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP), and EU GDPR guidelines. Full tenant data purge ("Right to be Forgotten") supported via automated tenant de-provisioning script.

### 7.3 Performance & Latency SLAs
* **Interactive Navigation:** P95 web application response time $< \mathbf{800\text{ ms}}$ for dashboards, search queries, and invoice reviews.

## 8. Matching Engine: Core Mathematical & Algorithmic Rules

### 8.1 Data Ingestion Entities
Let an active matching job evaluate:
* $\mathcal{I}$: Invoice with header metadata and lines $\mathcal{I}_1, \dots, \mathcal{I}_m$.
* $\mathcal{P}$: Purchase Order with header metadata and lines $\mathcal{P}_1, \dots, \mathcal{P}_n$.
* $\mathcal{G}$: Set of all Goods Receipts notes $\mathcal{G}^{(1)}, \dots, \mathcal{G}^{(k)}$ linked to $\mathcal{P}$.
* $\mathcal{H}$: History of previously approved or paid invoices $\mathcal{H}^{(1)}, \dots, \mathcal{H}^{(p)}$ linked to $\mathcal{P}$.

### 8.2 Line Mapping Algorithm
For each invoice line $\mathcal{I}_j$:
1. **Pass 1 — Exact Item Code:** Match $\mathcal{P}_i$ where $\text{Normalize}(\mathcal{I}_j.\text{item\_code}) == \text{Normalize}(\mathcal{P}_i.\text{item\_code})$.
2. **Pass 2 — Normalized Description Matching:** If no code match, compute token-sort cosine similarity $S(\mathcal{I}_j.\text{desc}, \mathcal{P}_i.\text{desc})$. If $S \ge 0.88$, map to $\mathcal{P}_i$.
3. **Pass 3 — Manual Resolution:** If $S < 0.88$, line is unmapped $\rightarrow$ trigger `ITEM_NOT_ON_PO`.

### 8.3 Cumulative Quantity Evaluation
For each mapped pair $(\mathcal{I}_j, \mathcal{P}_i)$:
1. Calculate **Total Received Quantity** to date:
   $$Q_{\text{received}}(\mathcal{P}_i) = \sum_{g \in \mathcal{G}} g.\text{received\_qty}(\mathcal{P}_i)$$
2. Calculate **Prior Invoiced Quantity**:
   $$Q_{\text{prior\_invoiced}}(\mathcal{P}_i) = \sum_{h \in \mathcal{H}} h.\text{invoiced\_qty}(\mathcal{P}_i)$$
3. Calculate **Net Billable Quantity**:
   $$Q_{\text{billable}}(\mathcal{P}_i) = \max\Big(0,\, Q_{\text{received}}(\mathcal{P}_i) - Q_{\text{prior\_invoiced}}(\mathcal{P}_i)\Big)$$
4. Check **Delivery Existence**:
   $$\text{If } Q_{\text{received}}(\mathcal{P}_i) == 0 \implies \mathbf{Raise\ Exception(GRN\_MISSING)}$$
5. Check **Over-Delivery vs Order**:
   $$\text{If } \Big(Q_{\text{prior\_invoiced}}(\mathcal{P}_i) + \mathcal{I}_j.\text{qty}\Big) > \mathcal{P}_i.\text{ordered\_qty} \times (1 + \text{Tol}_{qty\%}) \implies \mathbf{Raise\ Exception(QTY\_BILLED\_EXCEEDS\_ORDERED)}$$
6. Check **Over-Billing vs Physical Receipt**:
   $$\text{If } \mathcal{I}_j.\text{qty} > Q_{\text{billable}}(\mathcal{P}_i) \times (1 + \text{Tol}_{qty\%}) \implies \mathbf{Raise\ Exception(QTY\_BILLED\_EXCEEDS\_RECEIVED)}$$

### 8.4 Unit Price Evaluation
For each mapped pair $(\mathcal{I}_j, \mathcal{P}_i)$:
$$\Delta P = \mathcal{I}_j.\text{unit\_price} - \mathcal{P}_i.\text{unit\_price}$$
* If $\Delta P > 0$ and $\Delta P > (\mathcal{P}_i.\text{unit\_price} \times \text{Tol}_{price\%})$ AND $\Delta P > \text{Tol}_{price\_abs}$:
  $$\implies \mathbf{Raise\ Exception(PRICE\_HIGHER)}$$

### 8.5 Partial Deliveries & Backorder Support
If an invoice is presented for a partial delivery (e.g., PO ordered 100 units; GRN received 40 units; Invoice bills 40 units):
* $Q_{\text{billable}} = 40 - 0 = 40$.
* Invoice bills $40 \rightarrow$ Exact Match! Invoice is cleared for $\$40 \times \text{Price}$.
* Remaining open balance on PO line: $60$ units.
* When subsequent GRN of 60 arrives and subsequent invoice of 60 arrives:
  $$Q_{\text{billable}} = (40 + 60) - 40 = 60$$
  Subsequent invoice clears automatically.
